# Third-party software and assets

The root [Apache-2.0 licence](LICENSE) applies to the original Aether01 firmware code by DarkThunder. Third-party files and their modifications keep the licence and notices applicable to those files. The [hardware and enclosure repository](https://github.com/DT-DT001/Aether01-Hardware-AC101S) carries its own design licences and credits.

| Component | Source and licensing |
| --- | --- |
| Nofrendo NES core | Matthew Conte and other upstream contributors. See the [NES provenance and licence breakdown](components/aether_nes/LICENSES.md) and the [GNU Library General Public License, version 2](LICENSES/LGPL-2.0-only.txt). |
| minimp3 | CC0-1.0. See the [upstream notice](components/aether_audio_player/vendor/minimp3/UPSTREAM.txt) and [licence](components/aether_audio_player/vendor/minimp3/LICENSE). |
| minimp4 | CC0-1.0. See the [upstream and modification record](components/aether_mp4_player/vendor/SOURCE.md). |
| stb_image PNG decoder | Sean Barrett and contributors, under the MIT licence. The version pinned by LVGL carries the full notice in `src/libs/gltf/stb_image/stb_image.h`. |
| FatFs and ESP-IDF glue | ChaN's FatFs notices remain in its source files. Espressif glue and the local overlay retain their applicable licences. See [provenance](components/fatfs/PROVENANCE.json) and [licence](components/fatfs/LICENSE). |
| USB Host audio and mass storage | Apache-2.0 vendor components with local changes. See the [UAC provenance](components/aether_usb_audio/vendor/usb_host_uac/PROVENANCE.json) and [MSC provenance](components/aether_usb_storage/vendor/usb_host_msc/PROVENANCE.json), and the included licence files. |
| ESP-SR and NSNet3 | Offline recording noise suppression from Espressif's ESP-SR 2.6.2 component. Use on Espressif products is covered by the [Espressif MIT licence](LICENSES/ESP-SR.txt). The NSNet3 weights are included via that component's separate model image. |
| UI bitmap fonts | Derived from Huayuan Gothic by Lapomme under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. See the [font provenance and licence](main/assets/FONTS.md). |
| Pinyin character candidates | Adapted from the LVGL 9.5 single-syllable dictionary under MIT and expanded with Unicode 17.0.0 Unihan Mandarin readings. See the [LVGL licence and notice](main/assets/pinyin_candidates.inc) and the [Unicode licence and source](main/assets/pinyin_unihan.LICENSE.txt). |
| Pinyin vocabulary and frequencies | Based on Android PinyinIME by The Android Open Source Project under Apache-2.0, with phrase-pinyin-data word readings and jieba frequencies under MIT. See the [Android source and NOTICE](main/assets/pinyin_aosp_words.LICENSE.txt) and [supplemental sources and notices](main/assets/pinyin_words_extra.LICENSE.txt). |
| Flash-resident Pinyin vocabulary extension | Derived from [Wanxiang's simplified base dictionary by amzxyz](https://github.com/amzxyz/rime-wanxiang-data/blob/581925b26a89cd86387e20ffbd0ba746f89a81c9/dicts/jichu.dict.yaml) under [CC BY 4.0](main/assets/pinyin_extension.LICENSE.txt). Aether01 retains words covered by its fonts, normalizes their pronunciations, removes duplicates and packs the selected entries with quantized weights. See [upstream license scope](https://github.com/amzxyz/rime-wanxiang-data/blob/581925b26a89cd86387e20ffbd0ba746f89a81c9/LICENSE-SCOPE.md) and [the generator](tools/generate_pinyin_extension.py). |

ESP-IDF, LVGL and other managed dependencies are resolved by the build environment and its component manager. Their own licences apply. In particular, the [ESP Audio Codec licence](components/aether_mp4_player/LICENSE.esp_audio_codec) limits use to Espressif products. [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/DT-DT001/Aether01-AC101S-/releases) provides firmware images for the ESP32-S31-based Aether01 AC101S, the corresponding source tags, and a licence and attribution archive.
